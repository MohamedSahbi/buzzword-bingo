How it works
  1. Choose a topic or join a room.
  2. Click a square when you hear the phrase.
  3. Get five across, down, or diagonally.
  4. Shared Board gives everyone one card; Individual Cards gives each player a different card.
  5. The host can start a new card or reset the game for everyone.
